Markets Monitor

A personal project tracking equities, rates and FX across four regions, with my own weekly summary. Indices are shown through US-listed ETFs that track them (e.g. SPY for the S&P 500), because index levels are licensed data.

Data and sources

Prices and FX: Twelve Data. US rates, ECB rate, Brent and OECD 10-year yields: FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. UK rates: Bank of England. US CPI: US Bureau of Labor Statistics via FRED. Japan CPI: OECD. Monthly yields are monthly averages; prices may be delayed.
